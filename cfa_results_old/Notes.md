
## What these runs taught us

1. **Old cyclist respecification:** fit improved a lot (CFI .871 to .919 to .939), but the overlap between ATT, PBC and HAB did not go down (max correlation .90, .89, .93). The gain came mostly from dropping sn5, pbc7 and hab7.
2. **Pedestrian M1:** added reversed items had loadings near zero or negative if the wording allows.
3. **Driver M1:** sn4_r loaded about .20 and cross-loaded on intention. It does not belong to the norm scale.
4. **Motorcyclists:** sn5 cross-loaded on PBC (modification index 102).

These findings led to the current item decisions in `cfa_results/`.
